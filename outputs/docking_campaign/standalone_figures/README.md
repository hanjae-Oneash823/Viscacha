# Standalone docking figures

These are independent assets, not finished slides. PNG files are ready for PowerPoint; SVG/PDF versions of plots and schematics remain editable and scale without pixelation.

## Strongest figures to use

- `FYN_redocking_RMSD_replicates.*` — the primary validation plot: 9/9 top poses are below 2 Å.
- `3D/FYN_pocket_pose_overlay.png` — the clearest molecular view of pose recovery.
- `FYN_domain_architecture.*` — explains why the validated canonical FYN pocket is not present in the predicted 115-residue alternate product.
- `KIT_masitinib_score_replicates.*` — reproducibility of the canonical KIT baseline.
- `KIT_1T46_structure_coverage.*` — explains why a KIT-223 score is not reported.

## Additional 3D assets

- `3D/FYN_overview_crystal_vs_docked.png` — full FYN structural context.
- `3D/FYN_pocket_surface_overlay.png` — translucent-pocket alternative.
- `3D/KIT_overview_docked_masitinib.png` — full canonical c-KIT context.
- `3D/KIT_pocket_docked_masitinib.png` — close view of docked masitinib.

All 3D PNGs are 3000 × 2400 with transparent backgrounds.

## Molecular color key

- Teal: crystallographic saracatinib
- Orange: best docked saracatinib pose
- Purple: docked masitinib
- Blue-gray: protein cartoon
- Pale sticks: nearby protein side chains

## Interpretation boundary

The FYN result validates recovery of a known crystallographic pose. The KIT result is a stable canonical baseline only. Docking scores are not experimental affinities and do not establish cellular efficacy or disease causality.
