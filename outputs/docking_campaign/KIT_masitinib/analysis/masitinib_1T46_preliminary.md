# c-KIT–masitinib preliminary docking

Masitinib was docked 9 times into the validated experimental c-KIT 1T46 ATP pocket. The Vina score distribution was -12.82 ± 0.03 kcal/mol (mean ± SD); median -12.81 kcal/mol.

This supports a reproducible canonical c-KIT docking setup only. It must not be interpreted as an effect of KIT-223, because residue 715 is in an unresolved kinase-insert segment of 1T46.
